title: "Sacrifice"

---

Canonical documentation:
[Sacrifice](https://docs.opencoven.ai/docs/cli/sessions#sacrifice).

Sessions with any launch adoption, input adoption, or historical reservation
are not eligible. Coven reports: `session adoption evidence is retained;
sacrifice is unavailable until an approved retention/fence contract resolves
it`. O3 does not provide a retention/fence release.
