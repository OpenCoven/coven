Hermes 1.0.3 is a current trusted opt-in recipe. Install the upstream CLI and
opt in with its named Coven recipe. The current maturity table does not
categorize it as a future harness.

Future adapter direction remains Aider, Cline, and Gemini CLI. These harnesses
have no bundled or trusted-recipe support claim until they complete the
standard adapter contract and maturity checklist.
